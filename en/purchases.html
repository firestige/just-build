Purchases & Subscription

Purchases & Subscription

Just Build combines free core tools, one-time unlocks, permanent add-on libraries, and the Studio subscription. Common parts stay accessible while higher-cost modeling work and ongoing services can be supported fairly.

Access Model

Common European-standard aluminum profiles and common connectors are part of the free standard library. Rare profiles, specialized connectors, and complex build scenarios may be offered as add-on libraries because they require extra measurement, modeling, and maintenance work.

Free Standard Library

Includes common profiles, common connectors, basic 3D planning, and local project preview.

Pro Unlock

A one-time purchase for BOM export, cut lists, project management, and local productivity features.

Add-on Libraries

Purchased profile, connector, or build-scenario libraries are permanent and can be added to projects at any time.

Studio Subscription

Unlocks AR, FEM structural analysis, and all released add-on libraries while the subscription is active.

Add-on Library Examples

Add-on libraries are not required for basic use. They cover more specialized or complex build contexts, such as rare profile series, heavy-duty connectors, linear motion components, height-adjustable desk mechanisms, lead screws, motors, and custom build kits.

Access After Subscription Ends

Add-on libraries accessed through Studio do not become permanent purchases automatically. After a subscription ends, add-on parts already placed in existing projects remain viewable and editable so those projects continue to open correctly.

After a subscription ends, add-on parts that were not purchased separately cannot be added from the library again. If such a part is deleted from a project, it cannot be re-added from the library unless you buy the add-on or restore the subscription. Undoing a recent delete remains allowed.

Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription

If you change devices, reinstall the app, or do not see a purchased entitlement, use Restore Purchases in the app. Subscriptions are managed by Apple, and you can view, renew, or cancel them in your Apple ID subscription settings.

Terms

Unless otherwise stated in the app, Just Build uses Apple's standard End User License Agreement. Purchases, subscriptions, and refunds are handled by the Apple App Store.
