Privacy Policy

Just Build Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Just Build iOS app handles your data. Last updated: May 6, 2026.

The current version of Just Build does not include user accounts, advertising SDKs, or third-party analytics SDKs. Project files are stored locally on your device by default unless you choose to export, share, or use a feature that requires online processing.

Data We Process

Just Build primarily processes the project data you create in the app, including aluminum profile members, connectors, dimensions, materials, loads, results, BOMs, cut lists, and exported files. This data is used to provide planning, preview, export, and support features.

Just Build does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photo library, precise location, or health data.

Local Storage

Project data is stored locally on your device by default so you can open, edit, and continue your projects. If you export or share a file using system features or app export tools, the receiving app or service handles that copy under its own privacy policy.

Optional Online Processing

If you actively use a feature that requires server-side processing, the app may send only the model parameters needed for that task, such as dimensions, materials, sections, constraints, and loads. We use this data to return results, troubleshoot service issues, and maintain service security.

If a specific app version does not enable online processing, this data is not sent to a backend service.

Support Requests

When you contact us by email or through GitHub Issues, the text, screenshots, logs, or sample files you provide are used to understand and resolve the issue. Please do not include sensitive personal information, trade secrets, or project files you do not want to share.

Third-Party Services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process access logs according to its own service policies. The app currently does not integrate advertising networks or third-party behavior tracking SDKs.

Retention and Deletion

You control project data stored locally on your device. You can delete projects in the app or remove the app and its data through iOS. Support request information may be retained for a reasonable period needed to resolve issues, maintain the product, and meet compliance needs.

Children's Privacy

Just Build is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Updates

We may update this policy when app features, data handling practices, or compliance requirements change. Updated policies will be posted on this page with a new update date.

Contact Us

For privacy-related requests, email support@firestige.xyz and include "Privacy" in the subject.
