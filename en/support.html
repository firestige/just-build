FAQ

Does Just Build automatically upload my projects?

No. Project files are stored locally by default. Data leaves your device only when you export, share, or use a feature that requires online processing.

Can planning results be used as final engineering approval?

Just Build is intended for early planning and build preparation. Projects involving safety, structural loads, commercial delivery, or regulatory requirements should be reviewed by qualified professionals.

How do I submit a privacy request?

Email support@firestige.xyz and include "Privacy" in the subject. Do not send unrelated sensitive information.
